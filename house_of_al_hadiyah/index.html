Handcrafted

Every bracelet is meticulously assembled by hand. Artisans hand-select each stone for color harmony, thread them on durable reinforced cording, and finish them with traditional hand-tied knotwork.

◈

Natural Stones

We use genuine, ethically gathered semi-precious gemstones—African turquoise, red jasper, moss agate, golden amber, and natural terracotta clay beads. No synthetic dyes or plastic composites.

♾

Adjustable Fit

Engineered with a sliding macramé tension closure that glides smoothly to fit wrists from 6.0" to 8.5". Tailored for effortless stacking, secure all-day wear, and tactile comfort.

The Brand Ethos
"True beauty isn't engineered in a machine. It is worn, lived in, and shaped by the earth."

House of Al Hadiyah was founded on the belief that everyday talismans should carry weight, story, and tactile warmth. We design bracelets that embrace organic variation, celebrate the irregularities of raw minerals, and look even better as time passes.
